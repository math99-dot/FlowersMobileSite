Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Do you offer same-day delivery?

A: Yes Orders placed before 2 PM can be delivered same-day.

Q: How far in advance should I order wedding flowers?

A: We recommend booking 6-8 months in advance, especially for May-October weddings.

Q: What is your delivery area?

A: We deliver within a 25-mile radius. Contact us for details.

Q: Can I customize an arrangement?

A: Yes! We love creating custom arrangements.

Q: How long will my flowers last?

A: Most fresh flowers last 5-7 days with proper care.

Q: Do you have a minimum order for events?

A: Yes, we have a $500 minimum for large events and weddings.

Q: What payment methods do you accept?

A: We accept all major credit cards, debit cards, and cash.

Q: Can I see my arrangement before it's delivered?

A: For custom orders and weddings, we can share photos for approval.
